## Two resolved blockers

Claude's re-review accepted the version 2 corrections for the two blocking findings against the earlier scoring milestone:

1. Coverage parsing now requires the exact `complete` discriminant and rejects missing, malformed, unknown, explicitly incomplete, insufficient, and contradictory evidence.
2. Potentially material transaction ambiguity now considers all matching answer-window legs after exact deduplication, including individually subthreshold legs, without treating their combined amount as a scored value or direction.

## Non-blocking improvements accepted for hardening

The re-review requested direct regressions for missing and nonnumeric `observedAtMs`, exact `$2,500` combined-leg ambiguity, negative and nonnumeric USD values, empty identifiers, invalid event addresses, and deterministic selection among multiple conflicts and simultaneous ambiguities.

It also recommended these focused contract changes:

- validate and lowercase Ethereum transaction hashes before duplicate and transaction grouping;
- reject every supplied event whose chain is not exactly normalized `ethereum`;
- reject a coverage claim whose end is later than `observedAtMs`;
- strengthen the future public serializer boundary to remove exact timestamps and USD values as well as identities, source IDs, answers, and evidence;
- present public time and value clues as relative times and size bands without claiming anonymity.

These improvements are accepted. Rules behavior is versioned to 3 because normalization and rejection behavior changes.
